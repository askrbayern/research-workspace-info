Personal research workspace

A small connection for everyday research.

Research connects the owner's local research tools to Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides, so selected notes, dataset catalogs and collaboration documents can be read and updated in place.

What it does

The connection supports finding research files, organizing submitted notes, maintaining a dataset catalog, and preparing or updating documents, spreadsheets and presentations. Work follows the owner's instructions and chosen research scope.

It is a personal tool maintained by askrbayern, not a public sign-up service. The Google authorization screen uses the app name Research; the local desktop OAuth client is research-ubuntu.

Google account access

The owner authorizes access through Google's consent screen. The selected permissions can cover files across the account; the workflow uses them for the research files and operations the owner requests. This static website does not sign anyone in or receive Google credentials.

Selected file content may be processed by the owner's configured AI tools to perform a requested task. See the privacy notice for access, storage, sharing and deletion details.

Contact
